Background & Research

About Me

I am an Electrical and Computer Engineering (ECE) Ph.D. candidate at the University of Maryland (UMD), conducting research in partnership with the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST). My academic focus centers on quantum hardware fabrication, high-frequency spin manipulation lines, and automated scanning tunneling microscopy (STM) lithography.

Prior to my doctoral studies, I served as an Aerospace & Systems Engineer at Voyager Technologies (formerly Space Micro Inc.) from February 2021 to March 2024. In that role, I engineered, tested, and validated flight-ready software-defined radios (SDRs) for small satellite constellations, contributing directly to space payloads deployed on orbit via SpaceX Falcon 9 missions.

My professional and research background bridges low-level hardware design—including FPGA HDL, microcontroller firmware, and RF test rigs—with advanced quantum control systems and automated laboratory measurement pipelines.
